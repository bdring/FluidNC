FluidNC Demonstration

Real FluidNC/src, real setup()/loop() on a background thread. A minimal config.yaml and a sample SD card are staged into MEMFS's /native_localfs and /native_sd before boot, then locked read-only. Input goes through a real xterm.js terminal straight to FluidNC's own Lineedit (src/lineedit.h) -- the same local echo, arrow-key/backspace editing, history, and $-setting tab completion a real serial console gets, including its real ANSI color escapes for completion highlighting, not a page-side reimplementation of any of that.
